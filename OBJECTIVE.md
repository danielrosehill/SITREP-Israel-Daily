# Report objective

Drafted 2026-11-25 from the owner's feedback on the first two editions. Items under "Assumed" are not yet confirmed.

- **Reader:** one informed reader in Israel, on paper at their desk each morning. Newsletter and audio audiences are secondary.
- **Purpose:** know what changed in the past 24 hours in Israel's security and diplomatic position and, above all, what it means.
- **Cadence and window:** daily at 08:00 Israel time, previous 24 hours.
- **Length:** a few A4 pages, single-sided; about four.

## Key questions

1. What is the bottom line?
2. What does the pattern mean, and what is the counter-reading?
3. What happened on each front that matters?
4. What is likely next, and what should be watched today?

## Earns space

Assessment first, then under-reported but credible developments. Cut first: marginal fronts, detail the headlines already gave, sources in the printed copy, the timeline (exceptional days only).

## Evidence bar

Whitelisted sources with reliability ratings. State and proxy media are attributed, never narrated as fact. Every URL kept in the run must come from the evidence. No meaning-changing word substitutions.

## Style

Title "SITREP Israel for <date>". Boxed bottom line; coloured region labels with the headline on its own line; bold key phrases; page breaks; running header and page x of y. No military markings, UTC line, precedence or severity.

## Success test

- The bottom line is clear in 15 seconds and the whole in about five minutes.
- The assessment says something the headlines did not.
- Each front is a headline and two to four bullets.
- No claim is stronger than its source.
- The pages look deliberate: no near-empty pages or orphan headings.

## Assumed

- Five-minute reading time, about four pages.
- Regional events with no Israel angle stay out of the World sections (inherited rule).
- The newsletter keeps its longer form.
