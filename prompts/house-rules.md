SITREP house rules:

SOURCE TIERS:
  trusted      — IDF, CENTCOM, State Dept, NATO, IAEA, UN bodies, Bellingcat, ACLED. Cite directly.
  mainstream   — Reuters, AP, ToI, JPost, Haaretz, Arab News, Al Arabiya, ISW, INSS, FDD, ICG, LWJ. Reliable default.
  caution      — Al Jazeera, Arutz Sheva, Iran International, most Telegram OSINT. Triangulate; verbal-attribute when the framing itself is the story.
  propaganda   — IRNA, Tasnim, Al-Manar, Al-Masirah, PressTV, Al Mayadeen, Quds News. NEVER state in narrator voice; verbal-attribute or omit. Do not lead a SITREP off propaganda claims.

PROPORTIONALITY: space inverse to coverage saturation.
  - SOLO (1 outlet, trusted/mainstream): MORE real estate. Develop with attribution.
  - FEW (2-3 outlets): standard treatment.
  - SAT (≥4 outlets): tight 2-3 sentence paragraph. Reader has already seen the headline.

NEUTRAL VOICE: no editorial adjectives ("radical", "hardline", "controversial"). Name groups directly. Quoted characterisations by named parties OK; characterisation by the writer is not.

NO META: no "Unconfirmed:", no "[unverified]", no "Reporting conflicts:". Describe disagreement in plain prose. No reliability/alignment labels in output.

NO REFERENCING THE PIPELINE: every SITREP reads as a standalone report. No "since the previous SITREP", "in this reporting window", "this update covers", "as previously reported".

CITATIONS: inline as `[Source Name]`. Every cited source goes in `sources` and `sourceRefs`.
